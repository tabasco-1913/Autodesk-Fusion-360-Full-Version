<a id="overview"></a>

**Autodesk Fusion 360** is a comprehensive cloud-based tool designed for collaborative industrial and mechanical design. Available on Windows, it seamlessly integrates CAD, CAM, and CAE functionalities, allowing users to design, test, and manufacture objects from anywhere. The **latest version** is packed with advanced features that enhance your productivity and creativity, making it an excellent choice for professionals in design and engineering.

## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **T-Spline Technology** — Sculpt and model designs with unparalleled flexibility.
- **Solid, Parametric, and Grid Modeling** — Create complex geometries effortlessly.
- **Simulation Capabilities** — Conduct thermal stress and linear static stress tests on your designs.
- **File Compatibility** — Import and export in multiple popular CAD formats including SLDPRT, SAT, IGES, STEP, STL, and OBJ.
- **Dynamic Design Review** — Analyze the adaptability of your designs in motion.

### Advanced Features:
- **Machining Options** — Support for 2, 2.5, and 3-axis machining.
- **3D Printing Tools** — Streamlined tools for preparing designs for 3D printing.
